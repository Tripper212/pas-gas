Mac App Store purchase required. These plug-ins require the PAS GAS standalone application to have been purchased and installed through the Mac App Store on the same Mac.

Installing

  1. Purchase and install the PAS GAS standalone application from the Mac App Store.
  2. Open the standalone application once, then close it.
  3. Download and open the AU + VST3 installer above.
  4. Follow the macOS Installer steps. An administrator password may be required.
  5. Open your DAW. If it was already running during installation, restart it once so it can scan the new plug-ins.
  6. Load PAS GAS from your DAW's Audio Unit or VST3 instrument list. The plug-in checks the Mac App Store purchase automatically.

Installed locations

Audio Unit: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/PAS GAS.component
VST3: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/PAS GAS.vst3

Compatibility

Apple-silicon Mac, macOS 13 Ventura or later, and an Apple-silicon AU or VST3 instrument host. Ableton Live and Logic Pro are supported.

# PAS GAS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

LunaSound publishes PAS GAS and respects your privacy. PAS GAS does not sell, rent, use for advertising or use for tracking any customer data.

## Data collection

PAS GAS does not require a LunaSound account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking or telemetry.

Audio and MIDI are processed locally and in real time on your Mac. PAS GAS does not upload or transmit your audio, MIDI performances or project content.

## Optional plug-in entitlement service

The Mac App Store application is a complete standalone instrument. If you choose to download or use the optional Audio Unit and VST3 plug-ins, PAS GAS contacts the LunaSound entitlement service hosted by Wix to verify the associated App Store purchase.

For this purpose, PAS GAS sends an Apple-signed app transaction and a SHA-256 hash of the local App Store receipt. The service verifies the purchase and its current refund or revocation status with Apple. It stores the receipt hash, Apple app transaction identifier, App Store environment, a hashed activation token, and record creation and update times. This information is used only to provide the optional installer and signed plug-in entitlement, renew that entitlement, prevent unauthorized access, and respond to refunds or revocations.

The entitlement service does not receive your name, email address, postal address, audio, MIDI, presets or project content from PAS GAS. Apple and Wix may process technical connection information needed to provide and secure their services under their respective privacy terms.

Entitlement records are retained while needed to provide and verify an eligible purchase, prevent fraud, honour refunds or revocations, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact LunaSound about an entitlement record or request its deletion. Deleting an active entitlement record may prevent the optional plug-ins from being authorized until PAS GAS verifies the purchase again.

## Data stored on your Mac

PAS GAS can store presets, application preferences and MIDI-device selections locally on your Mac. A DAW may also store PAS GAS plug-in state inside its own project or session files. This local information is not transmitted to LunaSound by PAS GAS.

You can remove locally saved presets and settings from your Mac and uninstall PAS GAS at any time.

## TestFlight and the App Store

If PAS GAS is distributed through TestFlight or the Mac App Store, Apple may process installation information, diagnostics, usage metrics and feedback that you voluntarily submit. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and service terms. PAS GAS itself does not add independent analytics or tracking.

## Support messages

If you contact LunaSound support, LunaSound receives the email address and information that you choose to include in your message. This information is used only to respond to your request and diagnose the reported issue. Do not include confidential audio, project files or personal information unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if a future PAS GAS release changes how information is handled. The current policy will remain published on the PAS GAS support site.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact LunaSound at [lunasound.info@gmail.com](mailto:lunasound.info@gmail.com).

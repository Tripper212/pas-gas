# PAS GAS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

PAS GAS is published by Luke Slater under LunaSound. This policy explains how the PAS GAS macOS application, its AU and VST3 plug-ins, and the related support and download services handle information.

## Audio, MIDI and project data

PAS GAS processes audio and MIDI locally and in real time on your Mac. It does not upload or transmit your audio, MIDI performances, presets, DAW projects or other creative content.

PAS GAS does not include advertising, third-party analytics, behavioural tracking or telemetry. It does not sell, rent or share personal information for advertising or marketing.

## Information stored on your Mac

PAS GAS may store presets, application preferences and MIDI-device selections locally on your Mac. A DAW may also store PAS GAS plug-in state in its own project or session files. PAS GAS does not transmit this locally stored information to LunaSound.

You can remove locally saved presets and settings and uninstall PAS GAS at any time.

## App Store purchase verification and plug-in downloads

The Mac App Store version may use Apple's StoreKit framework to confirm that the copy of PAS GAS was obtained through the App Store. Apple provides cryptographically signed app-transaction information for this purpose.

If you choose to download the companion AU and VST3 plug-ins, PAS GAS may send the minimum Apple-signed purchase proof needed to LunaSound's eligibility service. It is processed only to confirm entitlement and issue a short-lived installer download link. LunaSound does not receive your Apple Account password, payment-card details or the audio and project data described above. Purchase proof is not used for advertising, profiling or tracking.

The installer opens in your default web browser and may be delivered by a third-party hosting or content-delivery provider. Those providers may process ordinary network information, such as an IP address, browser type and request time, under their own privacy terms and security logs.

## Apple services

Apple processes App Store purchases, installation information and any diagnostics, analytics or TestFlight feedback that you choose to share with Apple. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and service terms. PAS GAS does not add independent analytics or tracking.

## Support messages

If you contact LunaSound support, LunaSound receives your email address and any information you choose to include. It is used only to respond to your request and diagnose the reported issue. Please do not send confidential audio, project files or personal information unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.

## Data retention and security

PAS GAS does not maintain a user-account database. Purchase-verification information is processed only as needed to provide and protect the requested download service. Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain support records and meet legal obligations.

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information handled by LunaSound, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Children's privacy

PAS GAS is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact LunaSound so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if a future PAS GAS release changes how information is handled. The current version will remain published on the PAS GAS support site, with its latest revision date shown above.
